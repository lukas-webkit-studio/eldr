Neon signs: a classic with a modern twist
Neon signs are a timeless classic with an enduring place in the history of illuminated advertising.
How is it made?
How do traditional neon signs work?
Classic neon signs are made of glass tubes shaped under heat which, once filled with gas, glow red. Other light colours are achieved by filling the tube with phosphor powder and argon. Each tube carries a welded-on electrode connected to a high-voltage transformer, insulated with a silicone sleeve.
Why do we recommend neon sign imitations instead?
Because they deliver a luxurious look at an affordable price. Traditional neon signs are beautiful, but they come with drawbacks such as a higher purchase price, demanding maintenance and high energy consumption. Modern neon sign imitations, however, offer the same visual effect at a substantially lower price.

Corten steel signage
A material with a natural rust patina that takes on a unique look over time and is ideal for façades or outdoor spaces.
Material: weathering steel (known as rusted steel) is highly durable and ideal above all for outdoor placement. Its distinctive appearance comes from the naturally rusty patina that develops over time, adding a unique aesthetic effect.
Placement: corten steel is used not only for advertising lettering but also for a range of design features such as logos, graphic patterns and decorative panels, which can go on building façades or into outdoor spaces.
