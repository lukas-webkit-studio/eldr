Pylons
Visible advertising mounted on a steel structure is eye-catching and visible even from greater distances, making it particularly effective.
Graphics: the pylon crown is the primary section where illuminated or non-illuminated advertising, text, or a logo can be placed. The crown can be single-sided, double-sided, triangular, or square.
Material: the pylon base is galvanised to minimise corrosion or treated with an anti-corrosion coating. The illuminated panels are fitted with energy-saving LED diodes.
Placement: signage for hypermarkets, shopping centres, company headquarters, and petrol stations. A concrete foundation is required for anchoring, as specified in the structural assessment. For improved safety and easier servicing, service platforms can be added.

Totems
Freestanding advertisements allow the placement of promotions in locations that require designation and navigation. The shape and design can be customised to suit your needs, offering broad promotional possibilities.
Graphics: the display area can feature text, logos, and images designed to capture maximum attention. Illumination is provided by energy-saving LED diodes. Totems can be shaped, curved, or enhanced with 3D lettering.
Material: Anchored in a concrete foundation. Smaller units can use an aluminium frame or a hot-dip galvanised steel frame. The cladding can be made of acrylic, composite material or aluminium sheets, to suit the customer’s requirements.
Placement: on access roads, at the entrances to complexes, petrol stations, department stores, or wherever signage and navigation are required. Can serve as an orientation system for companies and interiors.

Decades of custom totems and pylons
We have completed hundreds of projects and possess the experience needed to manage any challenge. A testament to this is our record-setting project: the tallest pylon in the Czech Republic—the 37-metre-high Hyundai pylon in Nošovice.
The design possibilities for totems and pylons are virtually limitless. We custom-manufacture every structure precisely according to the client's specifications, with advertising towers ranging in height from 1 to 40 metres.
Advertising pylons and totems from design to installation
We can deliver advertising pylons and totems as a complete turnkey project or take over at any stage of an existing project. We prepare the project design, visualisation, structural calculations, material certificates and production documentation. We also arrange lighting studies and inspections. During project preparation, we can use our lifting equipment to simulate the position and height of the totem on site.
