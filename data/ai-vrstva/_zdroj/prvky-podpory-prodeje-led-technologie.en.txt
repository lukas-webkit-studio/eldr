Sales promotion items
Promotional and advertising items are placed inside retail spaces. Their job is to catch the customer’s eye and increase their interest in making a purchase. They are known by the abbreviations POP (point of purchase) and POS (point of sale).
What we make: Stands, displays, eye-catchers, advertising totems, shelf decorations, leaflet holders, checkout stands, wall frames, shopping trolley decorations, shop window decals, bags and drink holders, stickers and more.
Finish: There is a vast choice of colour finishes and a wide range of materials to work with. Just tell us what you have in mind and we will make sales promotion items to measure.

LED displays
LED displays show the time, date, temperature or price (at petrol stations), for example. They are controlled by a remote control, an app or over a data network. They are also built into advertising totems. Choose from a wide range of colours and two types of display:
Segment displays: They show the digits 0 to 9 and text characters. Depending on the number of segments they come in four types: 7- and 9-segment displays show digits and some text characters, 14- and 16-segment displays show digits and the text characters of the Latin alphabet.
Matrix displays: They consist of an array of LEDs, usually grouped into a square matrix. On a square 8 × 8 LED matrix you can show simple graphics as well as numbers and text.
