Processing and sale of acrylic glass and polycarbonate
We cut material to an accuracy of 1/10 mm, with a cutting area of 2,000 × 4,000 mm. We keep most colours and sheet sizes in stock, so we can prepare acrylic glass to your dimensions while you wait.
Batch production: we handle batch production of acrylic covers, display stands and protective screens. Our solutions are used by joiners, opticians, banks and orchestras alike. We also work with manufacturers of interior fittings (furniture).
Technical facilities: we have a fully equipped “acrylic shop” with professional machinery, including vertical panel saws and sizing saws that guarantee first-class results.
Advanced processing techniques: we use professional acrylic glass techniques such as edge polishing with a diamond grinder, flame polishing of edges and heat forming the material in an oven.
