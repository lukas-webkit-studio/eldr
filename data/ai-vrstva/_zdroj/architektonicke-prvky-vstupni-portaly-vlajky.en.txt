Entrance portals
These are frames around the entrance door, in an illuminated or non-illuminated version, differing in design. Portals can incorporate logos, lettering or a message.
Materials: portals are built on an aluminium structure that can house LEDs. Depending on the lighting required, it is clad either in acrylic or in composite sheet material, which is non-illuminated.
Execution: a 3D sign can be mounted on the portal, or it can be made as an acrylic glass inlay. The portal can be illuminated, non-illuminated or fitted with raised lettering. Our team will create an individual design that respects what the building allows.
Placement: at the entrances to shops or company premises, to mark the way in clearly, attract attention and give visitors the information they need.

Architectural illuminated elements
We specialise in illuminated lines on building façades, façade lighting, dynamic advertising and façade bands. Our priority is to find the best technical and visual solution.
Materials: we use high-quality LED modules with a high IP rating – they stand up to the weather and ensure a long service life.
Execution: alongside the design we also handle technical details, secure fixings and adaptation to the particular space, materials and operating conditions. The result is a clean solution with high functionality and a long service life.

Advertising flags
We make textile advertising flags including flagpoles, ideal wherever there is no room for other types of advertising such as billboards or large display signs.
Execution: you can choose the shape of the flag to suit the location – we offer traditional rectangles as well as special shapes. Flags are made from quality materials that give them a long service life.
Placement: they work best where large numbers of people pass by, such as city streets, shopping centres, festivals or trade fairs. Because they move in the wind, flags can draw even more attention, which makes them more effective.
