Illuminated projecting signs
Advertising that shines day and night. LED backlighting ensures consistent visibility in bad weather and after dark.
Graphics: The face of the sign is covered with printed or plotter-cut graphics. Both the shape of the sign and the graphic design can be adapted exactly as you like, including custom shapes.
Material: The frame is an aluminium structure and the face is made of backlit acrylic glass or another sheet material. Brackets for fixing the sign to the wall are welded to the inner frame.
Suitable for: Premises that need to be seen from every side – shops on busy streets, restaurants, hotels and anywhere advertising has to attract attention after dark.

Custom-shaped projecting signs
Visible advertising on a steel structure is unmissable even from far away – and that is what makes it effective.
Graphics: The shape of the sign follows the silhouette of your trade’s symbol – a tooth, scissors, a heart, a key, a glass or anything a customer instantly connects with your business.
Material: The frame is an aluminium structure and the face is made of backlit acrylic glass or another sheet material. Brackets for fixing the sign to the wall are welded to the inner frame.
Suitable for: Businesses with an iconic symbol instantly linked to their trade – dental surgeries (a tooth), hairdressers (scissors), cafés (a cup), wine shops (a vine), credit unions (a key).

Pharmacy signs
They are protected by copyright law and provide uniform signage for pharmacies and healthcare facilities. We offer a wide range of illuminated and non-illuminated “Pharmacy” signs, decals for shop windows, floors and counters, and other signage.
Licence: We are one of the few companies licensed to produce pharmacy crosses. Working with Ing. arch. Flašar has allowed us to take part in producing a great many of them.
Range: Illuminated and non-illuminated “Pharmacy” signs, decals for shop windows, floors and counters, and further supplementary pharmacy signage.
Suitable for: Signage for pharmacies and for doctors’ surgeries.
