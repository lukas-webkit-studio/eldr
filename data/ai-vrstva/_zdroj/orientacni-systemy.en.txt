Wayfinding systems to suit the space and the need
We design indoor, outdoor and modular wayfinding systems so that they adapt easily to a particular place and to individual requirements.
What is a wayfinding system for?
It is a set of information boards, direction signs and labels that makes it easier for people to get around. It helps visitors quickly grasp how the space is laid out and find the right way without having to ask staff.
What are wayfinding systems made of?
From sheet material in aluminium, Dibond or acrylic. A popular material with a strikingly attractive look is anodized aluminium. Other materials can be used as well — wood, stainless steel, acrylic glass.
