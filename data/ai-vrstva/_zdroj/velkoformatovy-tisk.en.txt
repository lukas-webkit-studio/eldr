Large-format digital printing
We often combine digital printing with plotter-cut graphics to get the best possible result in both looks and durability.
Materials: we can print on paper, self-adhesive film, translucent (backlighting) film, perforated film, banner and textile materials.
Processing: latex digital printing that reproduces any design and colour combination down to the smallest detail. We laminate the prints with a gloss or matt laminating film, which increases resistance above all outdoors. We guarantee the colour fastness of our prints for at least three years.

Quality and service life come first
We run modern large-format printing on our own latex printer and finish it with protective lamination, which gives us rich colours and lasting resistance even outdoors.
How it’s made
Modern technology
We own a modern printer built for digital printing of large-format advertising and banners. We work with latex printing technology. It achieves full, rich colours that stay true for years, even with detailed artwork and demanding colour combinations.
Maximum durability
We laminate our prints, which ensures maximum colour durability, above all on prints outdoors that are exposed to constant sunlight. Lamination also protects the advertisement against mechanical damage, and you can choose between a gloss or matt laminating film.

Plotter-cut graphics
By plotter-cut graphics we mean lettering cut out of self-adhesive film. Its advantage is a long service life. It suits every type of advertising and information lettering.
Materials: coloured self-adhesive films suitable for application to a wide range of surfaces. The films we use stay colour-fast for up to seven years.
Processing: the lettering is cut to the required shape on a plotter and then applied to the base surface – which can be almost any surface with sufficient adhesion. It cannot produce colour gradients or more complex motifs at the quality digital printing achieves. Where needed, we therefore combine the two techniques.

Custom-made
Decals
Advertising decals for vehicles, shop windows, glazing and floors – in printed as well as plotter-cut graphics.
Vehicle and shop window decals: full and partial vehicle wraps as well as standalone lettering. We prepare the artwork together with you and take care of professional application. We produce shop window and glazing decals using printed or plotter-cut graphics – for advertising messages, information texts, opening hours, photographs and logos.
Perforated and privacy window films: perforated film limits the view into the interior while the view out is preserved and little daylight is lost. Privacy window film blocks the view in from outside – most often we use sandblast-effect film, which lets light through but limits visibility through the glass. Motifs can be cut into the film, which comes in a range of shades.
Floor decals and safety features: self-adhesive anti-slip films put floor space to effective use for advertising and suit business premises as well as public spaces. Safety features for the visually impaired help people with low vision move around safely.

Design wall art
We design and produce large-format pictures and wall graphics for restaurants, cafés, retail and corporate spaces – from stylish illustrations to bold brand motifs.
Materials and printing: we print on canvas, films and rigid boards such as Dibond or PVC, and supply the pictures in aluminium or wooden frames or frameless.
Processing: we take care of the artwork, precise high-resolution large-format printing and professional installation – everything tailored to the particular space, its light and the brand identity.
The result: a visual element that enhances the atmosphere, supports the brand and turns the interior into a place customers remember and photograph.
