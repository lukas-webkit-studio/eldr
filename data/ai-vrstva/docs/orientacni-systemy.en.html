What is a wayfinding system for? [zdroj]

It is a set of information boards, direction signs and labels that makes it easier for people to get around. It helps visitors quickly grasp how the space is laid out and find the right way without having to ask staff. [zdroj]

What are wayfinding systems made of? [zdroj]
